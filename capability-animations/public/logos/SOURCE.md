# Channel logos

Paths are unmodified from Simple Icons 16.38.1 (CC0 1.0), which sources
them from Meta's brand resources. The official Meta asset pages could not be
reached from the build environment, so these were used as the fallback.

Fills:
* WhatsApp: #25D366 (official green). The phone is the path's cut out, so it
  shows white on the white logo tile.
* Instagram: linear gradient #FFD600, #FF7A00, #FF0069, #D300C5, #7638FA,
  bottom left to top right.
* Messenger: linear gradient #00B2FF to #0866FF, top to bottom.

The marks are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the
channels.
